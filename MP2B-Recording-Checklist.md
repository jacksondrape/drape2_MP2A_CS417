2. Select the empty sale cart to demonstrate its lesson. Mine and collect ore; sell at least 50 Gold total. Show the soda introduction, then its shelf. Spending Gold does not erase sale progress.
3. Buy a machine, try the hammer before placement, then place it. Show First Build. Upgrade it once to earn Engineer, upgrade again, then show the max-level lesson.
